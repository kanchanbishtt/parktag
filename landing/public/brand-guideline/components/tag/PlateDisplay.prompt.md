The vehicle number, ParkTag's most recognisable element: Courier New, 900, wide tracking, white on a navy gradient with an inset highlight.

```jsx
<PlateDisplay>DL 8C AB 1234</PlateDisplay>
<PlateDisplay variant="dashed">Enter your number</PlateDisplay>
```

Never set a plate in the UI font, and never colour it red — the plate is always navy or the dashed placeholder.
