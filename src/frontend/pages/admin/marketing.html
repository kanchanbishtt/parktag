Scan to activate — this list keeps up on its own. Hand a sticker to the customer and let them activate it the normal way. The unit turns ACTIVATED here by itself. If they buy it, hit Sold. If they don't, hit Deactivate Tag and everything they entered is erased, leaving the sticker as new for the next person.
Two stickers, one tag. Each design was printed twice and both copies carry the same QR, so they are one unit with two bodies. Both copies must always go to the same person — hand them to two customers and the second one's scans will reach the first.
