Scan to activate. This list keeps up on its own.
Hand a sticker to the customer and let them activate it the normal way. The unit turns ACTIVATED here by itself. If they buy it, hit Sold. If they don't, hit Deactivate Tag and everything they entered is erased, leaving the sticker as new for the next person.
Copies share one QR. Every copy must go to the same person.
Where a row shows a copy count, that design was printed more than once and every copy carries the same QR, one unit with several bodies. Hand two copies to two customers and the second one's scans will reach the first. A unit with no copy count is a single sticker and has no such constraint.
