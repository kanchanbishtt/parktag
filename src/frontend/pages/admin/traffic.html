Traffic

Where landing-page visitors are coming from. Worked out on the server from each visitor's IP address, which is resolved to a city and then discarded. Nobody is asked for permission, nothing is stored in their browser, and no IP address or device fingerprint is kept, so this covers every visitor rather than only the few who would accept a location prompt.
